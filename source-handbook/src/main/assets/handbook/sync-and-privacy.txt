Your data stays on this device

Candela has no account and no cloud sync. There is nothing to sign up
for, and no Candela server ever receives your library, reading positions,
bookmarks, notes, highlights, pronunciation dictionary, settings or keys.

Cloud sync through InstantDB was removed in 2026 (issue #1821), when that
service was going away. The app now works exactly as it always did for
people who never signed in: fully offline, on this device.

What that means for you

Everything you do in Candela lives on this phone or tablet. Your
  library, follows, history, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings are
  stored in the app's own on-device database.
Nothing to lose by not signing in. There is no sign-in screen any
  more, and no feature is locked behind an account.
Features you turn on still talk to their service. If you sign in to
  Royal Road, AO3 or GitHub, or connect Google Drive, Notion, Slack or
  Discord, Candela talks directly to that service to fetch your stories, and
  those sign-ins are kept on this device, encrypted. Optional Azure cloud
  voices send the text being read to Azure, and AI chat with your own key
  sends your messages (and any book text you share) to that AI provider.

Moving to a new phone or reinstalling

Because there is no Candela cloud, a reinstall starts fresh unless your
phone restores it for you:

Android's own backup (Settings → System → Backup, under your Google
  account) can restore your library and settings onto a new phone. This is
  Android's feature, not Candela's, and you control it. For your safety it
  never includes your saved passwords and keys, the document wallet, Voice
  Notes, or deadline reminders; those stay on the original device only.
Export a book (the book's menu → Export as EPUB) to keep a copy of
  anything you have made or imported.

Privacy in one line

No Candela account and no Candela server: nothing is uploaded to us. Data
leaves your device only when a feature you turn on sends it directly to
the service you chose (a source you connect, Azure cloud voices, or AI chat
with your own key), or when Android backs it up under your own account.

See privacy.md for the full privacy policy.
